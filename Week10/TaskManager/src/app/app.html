📜 Game Rules & Mechanics

🎯 XP & Priorities

Every task you complete grants XP based on its priority level.

  • High Priority: 50 XP
  • Medium Priority: 30 XP
  • Low Priority: 10 XP

🔥 Daily Streaks & Multipliers

Complete at least one task a day to build your streak and earn bonus XP on every task!

  • 1-2 Days: 1.0x (Standard XP)
  • 3-6 Days: 1.5x Multiplier
  • 7+ Days: 2.0x Multiplier (On Fire!)

🧊 Streak Freezes

Life happens! If you miss a day of productivity, a Streak Freeze will automatically be consumed to prevent your streak from resetting to zero. You can purchase them from the top right menu for 500 XP each.

🎖 Titles & Levels

Your total lifetime XP determines your rank:

  • 0 XP: Procrastinator
  • 101 XP: Task Padawan
  • 501 XP: Productivity Ninja
  • 1500 XP: Task Lord
